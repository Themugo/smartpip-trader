Frequently Asked Questions

Is SmartPip free to use?
Yes! SmartPip offers a free plan that includes paper trading, basic analytics, and access to our learning center. Premium plans offer advanced features like multiple broker connections and priority AI processing.
Which brokers are supported?
Currently, SmartPip supports Deriv. We're actively working on adding more broker integrations. You can start using paper trading immediately without connecting a broker.
Is my trading data secure?
Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data. We never share your personal information or trading data with third parties. You can also enable two-factor authentication for extra security.
Can I practice without risking real money?
Yes! Our paper trading feature lets you practice with virtual funds using real market data. It's a great way to develop and test strategies before trading with real capital.
